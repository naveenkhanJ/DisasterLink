# Use Case: Escalate Warning
**Use Case Scenario:** On 21 May 2026 at 08:16, right after verifying a Kalutara citizen's report of the Kalu Ganga breaching its sandbag barriers, DMC Duty Officer K. Perera follows UC2's escalation prompt and raises the active warning WRN-2026-0087 for the Kalu Ganga flood hazard (HZ-2026-0142; covered districts Ratnapura and Kalutara) from Level 2 – Amber (Be Alert) to Level 3 – Red (Evacuate) with new evacuation instructions in Sinhala, Tamil and English and a new expiry time, and the citizens, response organisations and district officers of both districts are alerted again

*Primary actor: DMC Officer (a DMC Duty Officer can escalate, because DMC Duty Officer specialises DMC Officer). Secondary actors: District Officer (receives the escalated warning; associated with Escalate Warning on the use case diagram); through the included Broadcast Warning: Citizen, Resource-Owning Organisation, Push Notification Service, SMS Gateway (mobile operator). Extension points: none. Escalate Warning «include»s Broadcast Warning and «extend»s UC2 Submit and Verify Hazard Report at extension point `verified report affects an active hazard`.*

**Description:**
This use case scenario describes how a DMC Officer raises an active warning to a higher level on NBRO's three-level scale, with a new message, emergency instructions and expiry time in Sinhala, Tamil and English, and how the system re-broadcasts the escalated warning to the citizens, the response organisations and the district officers of the warning's covered districts, so that the district officers can coordinate the response. The escalation starts from UC2's escalation prompt when a verified report affects a hazard with an active warning (extension point `verified report affects an active hazard`; main flow) or from the warning dashboard (A1); in both cases it is a separate action that follows the officer's own choice — verifying a report never escalates a warning by itself — and it runs online only.

*Conventions:* The levels are NBRO's Level 1 – Yellow (Remain Vigilant), Level 2 – Amber (Be Alert) and Level 3 – Red (Evacuate). The escalation details form is part of the escalation screen, is pre-filled from the warning and keeps the values already entered whenever it is shown again; its "Proceed to preview" submits the details for validation and the preview. A server or network failure is shown by the screen's common error banner and changes nothing (E4); an escalation is never queued offline. An SMS carries the message followed by the instructions in the recipient's language, separated by one line break that counts as one character, so the SMS length limit (configured as in UC1: 201 characters for Sinhala and Tamil, 459 for English) applies to the two together; the English counter values are exact (124 + 1 + 134 = 259), while the Sinhala and Tamil values are estimates, because the scenario does not reproduce those texts. A hazard is labelled on screen by its type and location name ("Flood — Kalu Ganga at Ratnapura"); "Kalu Ganga flood" is the scenario's narrative name. The SMS Gateway (mobile operator) reaches subscribers through the mobile operators' DEWN gateway.*

**Preconditions:**
- The DMC Officer works at the DMC operations centre with a network connection (Escalate Warning, like UC1, stays online).
- The hazard has an active warning. In this instance the hazard is the Kalu Ganga flood hazard (HZ-2026-0142, shown as "Flood — Kalu Ganga at Ratnapura"; Flood, severity Medium, status ACTIVE, recorded from an Irrigation Department bulletin, districts Ratnapura and Kalutara, river basin Kalu) and its active warning is WRN-2026-0087, issued in UC1 Issue Hazard Warning by DMC Officer N. Jayasinghe on 20 May 2026 at 10:45: Level 2 – Amber (Be Alert), target district Ratnapura and river basin Kalu (covered districts Ratnapura and Kalutara), channels Push, SMS and Audible alert, message and emergency instructions in Sinhala, Tamil and English, expiry time 21 May 2026, 10:45.
- For the main flow, the DMC Duty Officer is deciding a pending citizen report in UC2 Submit and Verify Hazard Report (in this instance RPT-2026-KLT-0847).
- Citizens, response organisations and district officers are registered with their district (organisations with their service-area districts), contact number and preferred language; citizens with the app also have a device token. In Ratnapura and Kalutara these are 42,335 citizens (30,100 with the app), 14 response organisations and 4 district officers (2 per district).

**Main Flow:**
1. In UC2 Submit and Verify Hazard Report, DMC Duty Officer K. Perera verifies report RPT-2026-KLT-0847 from Asela Gunawardene, a citizen in Kalutara ("Kalu Ganga water has breached the sandbag barriers near the Kalutara bridge …"), and links it to the existing Kalu Ganga flood hazard (HZ-2026-0142) (21 May 2026, 08:15).
2. System records the verification (UC2's base behaviour), finds that the hazard has the active warning WRN-2026-0087, and — at extension point `verified report affects an active hazard` — displays the decision together with an escalation prompt naming the hazard and warning WRN-2026-0087 at Level 2 – Amber (Be Alert), valid until 21 May 2026, 10:45, with the choices Escalate and Not now. (A report verified as a new hazard has no active warning, so no prompt is shown.)
3. DMC Duty Officer K. Perera chooses Escalate (08:16); the escalation screen opens for warning WRN-2026-0087. The verification is already recorded and is not changed by what follows.
4. System retrieves the warning, checks that it is still active at the current time, and determines the levels above its current level; it displays the escalation options: current level Level 2 – Amber (Be Alert), allowed level Level 3 – Red (Evacuate).
5. DMC Duty Officer K. Perera chooses Level 3 – Red.
6. System displays the escalation details form, pre-filled from the warning: new level Level 3 – Red, the warning's current message and emergency instructions in Sinhala, Tamil and English, and its current expiry time 21 May 2026, 10:45, with a character counter per language for the message and instructions together against the SMS limit.
7. DMC Duty Officer K. Perera replaces the message and the instructions in the three languages with evacuation text (English message: "DMC FLOOD WARNING - Level 3 Red: Kalu Ganga above major flood level in Ratnapura and Kalutara. Evacuate low-lying areas now."; English instructions: "Leave now for the safety centre named by your Grama Niladhari. Take documents, medicines and drinking water. Do not cross flood water."; counters Sinhala 192 / 201, Tamil 199 / 201, English 259 / 459), sets the new expiry time 22 May 2026, 08:25 and chooses "Proceed to preview", which submits the details.
8. System validates the submitted escalation details (the message and instructions are present in all three languages; each language's message and instructions together fit the SMS limit; the expiry time is after the current time; the chosen level is above the warning's current level) and, as they are valid, displays the preview of the re-broadcast: hazard type Flood, new level Level 3 – Red (Evacuate), covered districts Ratnapura and Kalutara, the new message and emergency instructions in Sinhala, Tamil and English, channels Push, SMS and Audible alert, expiry time 22 May 2026, 08:25, and the recipient summary — 18,420 citizens in Ratnapura and 23,915 in Kalutara, 14 response organisations and 4 district officers.
9. DMC Duty Officer K. Perera reviews the preview and confirms the escalation.
10. System validates the confirmed details again (E3 10g), checks again that warning WRN-2026-0087 is still active and below Level 3 – Red, escalates it — level Level 3 – Red, the new message and instructions in the three languages, expiry time 22 May 2026, 08:25 (its ID, target district Ratnapura, river basin Kalu, channels and issue time are unchanged) — and records an audit record WARNING_ESCALATED with the escalating officer (K. Perera), the time (21 May 2026, 08:25), the warning's ID and the level change Level 2 – Amber → Level 3 – Red.
11. System re-broadcasts the escalated warning: it resolves the citizens, the response organisations and the district officers of the covered districts Ratnapura and Kalutara; for each recipient and each of the warning's channels the recipient can receive (citizens: push and audible alert when they have a device token, and SMS; organisations and district officers: SMS) it creates a notification of a new broadcast round in the recipient's preferred language with the new level, message and instructions (an SMS carries the message followed by the instructions), adds it to the warning, sends it through the gateway that supports that channel, and marks it DELIVERED or FAILED from the gateway's result.
12. System displays the escalated warning — WRN-2026-0087, Level 3 – Red, Ratnapura and Kalutara, expires 22 May 2026, 08:25 — with the broadcast summary of this round (102,553 notifications delivered: 30,100 citizens with the app × 3 channels, 12,235 citizens by SMS, 14 organisations and 4 district officers by SMS; none failed).
13. Citizens in Ratnapura and Kalutara receive the Level 3 – Red alert with the evacuation instructions on the citizen app (with the alarm sound) and by SMS; the response organisations and the district officers of both districts receive it by SMS; the use case ends.

**Alternate Flows:**

**A1 — Escalation started from the warning dashboard** (at step 1)
- 1a. Instead of following an escalation prompt in UC2, the DMC Officer opens the warning dashboard.
- 1b. System retrieves the assessable hazards (status ACTIVE; recorded from agency bulletins or from verified reports) and displays them.
- 1c. DMC Officer selects the Kalu Ganga flood hazard (HZ-2026-0142), shown as "Flood — Kalu Ganga at Ratnapura".
- 1d. System displays the hazard details together with its active warning WRN-2026-0087 (Level 2 – Amber, Ratnapura and Kalutara, expires 21 May 2026, 10:45) and its current dispatch status (102,549 notifications delivered), and, because the hazard has an active warning, offers Escalate for that warning.
- 1e. DMC Officer chooses Escalate on warning WRN-2026-0087; the escalation screen opens for that warning; continues at step 4.

**A2 — Escalation prompt dismissed** (at step 3)
- 3a. DMC Duty Officer chooses Not now on the escalation prompt.
- 3b. System closes the prompt; the report stays verified and the warning stays at Level 2 – Amber; nothing is sent or recorded for an escalation; the use case ends.

**A3 — No higher level available** (at step 4; when the selected warning is at Level 3 – Red)
- 4a. When the selected warning is already at Level 3 – Red, System finds that no level above it exists.
- 4b. System displays the warning with the notice that it is already at the highest level and offers no level choice; the warning is unchanged and nothing is sent or recorded; the use case ends.

**A4 — Officer chooses a different level after the preview** (at step 9; when the selected warning is at Level 1 – Yellow (Remain Vigilant), so that both Level 2 – Amber and Level 3 – Red are offered)
- 9a. DMC Officer chooses another of the offered levels instead of confirming.
- 9b. System displays the escalation details form for that level; the form keeps the message, instructions and expiry time already entered and changes only the level; continues at step 7 (the officer adapts the text for that level and previews again).

**A5 — Officer cancels the escalation** (at step 5, step 7 or step 9)
- 5a / 7a / 9c. DMC Officer cancels the escalation instead of choosing a level (step 5), instead of completing the details (step 7) or instead of confirming the preview (step 9).
- 5b / 7b / 9d. System closes the escalation screen; the warning keeps its level, message, instructions and expiry time, no notification is sent and no audit record is created; the use case ends.

**Postconditions:**
- Warning WRN-2026-0087 is at Level 3 – Red with the new message and emergency instructions in Sinhala, Tamil and English and the new expiry time 22 May 2026, 08:25, after which it expires automatically; it is still the hazard's only active warning, with the same ID, target district and river basin, channels and issue time.
- One new notification exists per recipient and receivable channel, held by the warning as a new broadcast round, each DELIVERED or FAILED, plus any resend notifications in the same round (E1); the notifications of the original Level 2 broadcast are kept.
- The citizens, the response organisations and the district officers of Ratnapura and Kalutara have been sent the escalated warning, so the district officers can coordinate the response.
- An audit record WARNING_ESCALATED records the escalating officer, the time, the warning and the level change (old and new level).
- The officer has seen the escalated warning with its broadcast summary, and any failed recipients per channel.
- If the use case ends without this officer's escalation (A2, A3, A5, E2, or E4 when the first attempt was not recorded), this use case has not changed the warning and has created no notification or audit record. After E4 with a lost reply, the escalation stands as in the postconditions above.

**Exception Flows:**

**E1 — Notification Service Failure** (at step 11)
- 11a. The SMS Gateway (mobile operator) does not accept the SMS notifications of 615 recipients in Ratnapura (a mobile operator outage); the push and audible-alert notifications are delivered.
- 11b. System marks those notifications FAILED and keeps the failed recipients' IDs per channel in the broadcast summary; the other notifications are sent and marked as usual.
- 11c. System displays the escalated warning with the broadcast summary (101,938 notifications delivered, 615 failed) and alerts the officer that the warning was not delivered on SMS to 615 recipients, showing per channel the recipients whose notification failed (SMS: the 615; Push and Audible alert: none) and any unreachable recipients (none); the screen makes no claim about who was reached on another channel.
- 11d. The warning stays at Level 3 – Red and the audit record stands. The escalation screen offers no resend: when the SMS Gateway is available again, the officer selects the Kalu Ganga flood hazard (HZ-2026-0142) on the warning dashboard.
- 11e. System displays the hazard details with the active warning WRN-2026-0087 and its current dispatch status, covering the escalation round (101,938 delivered, 615 failed on SMS).
- 11f. DMC Officer chooses SMS as the channel for the failed recipients and resends (SMS again once the SMS Gateway is available, or Push, which reaches only citizens with the app — as UC1 Issue Hazard Warning, E1 16d).
- 11g. System sends the escalated warning by SMS (message followed by instructions) to the recipients with a recipient–channel pair whose latest notification in the current (escalation) round is FAILED, and who have no DELIVERED notification on SMS in that round — the 615 recipients; all 615 are delivered. System records each delivery status and displays the updated dispatch status: 101,938 + 615 = 102,553 notifications delivered, no failures, nobody unreachable (as UC1 E1 16e); the use case ends.

**E2 — Warning can no longer be escalated** (at step 4 or step 10)
- 4c / 10a. System finds that warning WRN-2026-0087 is no longer active — when the officer reaches step 4 after the warning's expiry time (21 May 2026, 10:45), or when it expires before step 10 — or, at step 10, that it is no longer below the chosen level because another officer's escalation to Level 3 – Red was recorded after the re-validation of E3 10g.
- 4d / 10b. System refuses the escalation, changes nothing, and displays the warning with the reason it cannot be escalated (warning expired, or its current level); no notification is sent and no audit record is created.
- 4e / 10c. The use case ends; for a warning escalated by another officer, the officer reviews the escalated warning; for an expired warning, the officer may issue a new warning in UC1 Issue Hazard Warning.

**E3 — Invalid escalation details** (at step 8; at step 10 when the confirmed details are validated again)
- 8a. System's validation finds invalid or missing details: the Tamil message and instructions together have 228 characters, more than the 201 of the SMS limit, and the Sinhala instructions are empty.
- 8b. System displays the validation messages beside the fields ("Tamil message and instructions: 228 / 201 characters", "Sinhala instructions are required"); no preview is shown, and the form keeps the details already entered.
- 8c. DMC Officer shortens the Tamil instructions, enters the Sinhala instructions and chooses "Proceed to preview" again; continues at step 8 (the details are validated again).
- 10g. If the details are no longer valid when confirmed — e.g. the new expiry time has passed while the preview was open, or the warning is no longer below the chosen level because another officer escalated it meanwhile — System shows the validation errors on the form, which keeps the details already entered, and the preview is not issued: the warning is not escalated by this officer, nothing is sent and no audit record is created; the officer corrects the details and continues at step 8; when the warning is already at or above the chosen level, the officer reviews the escalated warning instead (as E2 4e/10c) or cancels (A5 7a).

**E4 — Central system temporarily unavailable** (at step 10)
- 10d. The confirmation does not reach the central system, the system fails before the warning is escalated, or the reply is lost.
- 10e. System shows that the service is unavailable (the screen's common error banner): as far as the officer can tell, the warning stays at Level 2 – Amber and nothing was sent; the preview stays on the screen. The escalation is not queued offline (Escalate Warning runs online only).
- 10f. DMC Officer confirms again when the system is available; continues at step 10 (if the first attempt had in fact escalated the warning, the re-validation finds that it is no longer below Level 3 – Red and E3 10g shows this; the first attempt's escalation stands as in the postconditions).
