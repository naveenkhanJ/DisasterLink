# Use Case: UC2 Submit and Verify Hazard Report
**Use Case Scenario:** On the morning of 21 May 2026, Asela Gunawardene, a registered citizen in Kalutara District, reports the Kalu Ganga breaching its sandbag barriers at Kalutara North with one photo and his GPS location; DMC Duty Officer K. Perera verifies the report, links it to the active Kalu Ganga flood hazard (HZ-2026-0142) and is offered the escalation of that hazard's Level 2 – Amber warning WRN-2026-0087, which he raises to Level 3 – Red in Escalate Warning; Asela receives the decision as a push notice in Sinhala

*Primary actor: Citizen (including Community Volunteer, a kind of Citizen). Secondary actors: DMC Duty Officer, Location / GPS Service (through the included Capture GPS Location), Push Notification Service, SMS Gateway (mobile operator). Extension points: `network unavailable` (Save Data Offline, at step 18), `report rejected` (Reject Report, at step 25), `verified report affects an active hazard` (Escalate Warning, at step 29).*

**Description:**
This use case scenario describes how a Citizen or a Community Volunteer (a member of a Grama Niladhari-level Disaster Management Committee) reports an observed hazard from the citizen app — hazard type, description, one required photograph and the location, captured through the included Capture GPS Location (steps 12–14 and A1) or entered manually — and how a DMC Duty Officer at the DMC operations centre reviews the report, recorded as Pending verification, beside nearby reports and candidate hazards and then verifies it, linking it to an existing hazard or creating a new one, or rejects it with a mandatory reason. The reporter receives the decision as a push notice, or else an SMS, in their preferred language and can open a report-status screen; verification never issues or changes a warning, but when the linked hazard has an active warning the officer is offered Escalate Warning as a separate step, and when the phone has no network connection the report is kept on the phone as "Pending synchronisation" and sent automatically when the connection returns while the app is open, or as soon as the app is next opened with a connection (Save Data Offline).

*Conventions:* the push or SMS text of the notice is in the reporter's preferred language; a rejection reason is sent exactly as the officer typed it (officers are asked to write it in the reporter's language); the citizen chooses the language of the warnings on the home screen while the app is online; the app's own labels, including the report-status screen, stay in English (other languages are future work); notices and reasons quoted in this scenario are given in English translation. A hazard is labelled on the screens by its type and place name (`type` plus `locationName`, e.g. "Flood — Kalu Ganga at Ratnapura" — MD C2, FX-14); "Kalu Ganga flood" in this scenario is narrative. MD §0: **C1** — a server or network failure on an online screen (5xx, timeout, unreachable) is shown by the screen's common error banner, built in the web/app layer and not drawn; nothing is changed and the actor may retry; the report submission is offline-capable and follows the offline rules instead. **C2** — the hazard-type list, the report form, the rejection-reason field and its confirmation dialog are screen components, not dictionary displays; a form shown again keeps the values already entered. **C3** — the app screen holds the draft being edited and passes it to the draft validation and to the submission.

**Preconditions:**
- The reporter is registered as a Citizen (or Community Volunteer) with a registered district (Kalutara) and a preferred language, and the citizen app is installed on a phone that has a camera and GPS hardware.
- The DMC Duty Officer has the verification console open at the DMC operations centre, which has normal connectivity (the verification side stays online — CH-05).
- Hazards recorded from agency bulletins are available (Record and Monitor Hazard). In this instance, the Kalu Ganga flood hazard (HZ-2026-0142: Flood, severity Medium, location name "Kalu Ganga at Ratnapura", districts Ratnapura and Kalutara, river basin Kalu, source Agency bulletin (Irrigation Department), status Active) has an active warning, WRN-2026-0087: Level 2 – Amber (Be Alert), issued in Issue Hazard Warning on 20 May 2026 at 10:45 for the district Ratnapura and the Kalu basin (covered districts Ratnapura and Kalutara) and valid until 21 May 2026 10:45.

**Main Flow:**
1. Citizen opens the citizen app.
2. System displays the home screen. It shows the language selector (current language English), the active warnings for the citizen's district in that language ("Level 2 – Amber (Be Alert) — Flood — Kalu Ganga at Ratnapura (Ratnapura, Kalutara) — valid until 21 May 2026 10:45"), the citizen's own reports with their statuses (My reports), the current location (its coordinates with the GPS accuracy), the connectivity status "Online" and the Report Hazard option.
3. Citizen selects Sinhala in the language selector.
4. System records Sinhala as the citizen's preferred language and displays the home screen again with the warning's message and instructions in Sinhala.
5. Citizen selects Report Hazard.
6. System starts a new report draft on the phone, with a draft reference generated on the device and the capture time (21 May 2026, 07:40) recorded automatically. It then displays the hazard types Flood, Landslide, Cyclone, Tsunami, Drought, Blocked road and Other.
7. Citizen selects Flood.
8. System displays the report form (description, photo, location).
9. Citizen enters the description "Kalu Ganga water has breached the sandbag barriers near the Kalutara bridge. Flooding is now moving into the service road. Heavy continuous rainfall still active."
10. Citizen takes a photograph of the flooded road with the camera (or chooses one from the gallery).
11. System checks the draft's photograph — a readable JPEG or PNG image (accepted formats assumed by Group_058) — keeps a copy in the app's own storage and shows it as the report's only photo ("1 of 1"). A new photo would replace it. Only the photo checks are used at this point; the fields not yet filled in are checked at step 16.
12. System requests the current location from the Location / GPS Service.
13. Location / GPS Service returns latitude 6.5888 N, longitude 79.9640 E with an accuracy of 8 m.
14. System displays the location on a map with the coordinates and "GPS · accuracy ±8 m".
15. Citizen confirms the location by selecting Review Report.
16. System validates the draft: a hazard type is selected, the description is present and at most 500 characters (an assumed limit, from Group_060's storyboard counter "46/500"), there is exactly one readable photo, and the location lies within Sri Lanka. It then displays the review screen with the hazard type, description, photo, location (with its source and accuracy) and capture time, together with Edit and Submit Report.
17. Citizen reviews the report and selects Submit Report.
18. System sets the report's district from the location (Kalutara, resolved through the Location / GPS Service; the reporter's registered district would be used if the location could not be resolved), finds that the phone is online and sends the report, its photo and the submission details to the DMC server. The submission details are the draft reference as the submission ID, the capture time and the number of other drafts still Pending synchronisation on the phone (0; Rejected drafts are not counted).
19. System (server) validates the report again, confirms that this submission has not been received before and stores the photo. It records hazard report RPT-2026-KLT-0847 with the reporter (Asela Gunawardene), hazard type Flood, the description, the photo, the GPS location with its accuracy, district Kalutara, the capture time (07:40), the received time (07:42) and the status Pending verification. It also records the phone's last synchronisation.
20. System displays the confirmation screen in its online state: "Report submitted", report ID RPT-2026-KLT-0847, status "Pending verification" and Track report status.
21. DMC Duty Officer opens the verification queue on the verification console.
22. System displays the reports with the status Pending verification, oldest first. Each row shows the report ID, hazard type, district, capture time, reporter type (Citizen or Community Volunteer) and a View action. No row offers verify or reject.
23. DMC Duty Officer selects View on RPT-2026-KLT-0847.
24. System displays the report details:
    - the report: ID, reporter and reporter type, hazard type, description, photo, location with source and accuracy, district, capture time and received time;
    - the nearby reports, meaning other pending or verified reports of the same hazard type within 5 km received in the last 24 hours, not including this report (both values assumed by Group_058), each with its distance from this report: RPT-2026-KLT-0843 (Flood, Kalutara District, 07:05, 1.2 km, Pending verification) and RPT-2026-KLT-0845 (Flood, Kalutara District, 07:20, 2.8 km, Pending verification);
    - the candidate hazards, meaning active hazards of the same type whose districts include the report's district or that lie within 5 km of its location (assumed radius): "Flood — Kalu Ganga at Ratnapura" HZ-2026-0142 (severity Medium; Ratnapura, Kalutara; river basin Kalu; source Agency bulletin). The hazard's active warning is shown when the decision is recorded (step 29a).
25. DMC Duty Officer examines the evidence, judges the report valid, selects the candidate hazard "Flood — Kalu Ganga at Ratnapura" (HZ-2026-0142) and selects Verify and Link.
26. System records the verification. The report's status becomes Verified, the report is linked to the Kalu Ganga flood hazard (HZ-2026-0142), and the verifying officer (K. Perera) and the verification time (08:15) are recorded. The system then looks up the hazard's active warning and finds WRN-2026-0087.
27. Once the decision is recorded, System sends the decision notice to the reporter as a push notification through the Push Notification Service, in the reporter's preferred language (Sinhala). The notice gives the report ID, "Verified" and the decision time.
28. System displays on the console "Report verified" with the report ID, the linked hazard, the verifying officer and the verification time.
29. Extension point `verified report affects an active hazard`: the linked hazard has an active warning, so A7 runs (escalation prompt) and then continues at step 30. When the linked hazard has no active warning, the flow continues directly at step 30.
30. Citizen's phone receives the decision notice and displays it (in Sinhala; shown here in English translation): "Your report RPT-2026-KLT-0847 was verified by the DMC (21 May 2026, 08:15)".
31. Citizen opens the report from the notice (or from My reports).
32. System displays the report-status screen: report ID, hazard type, capture time, status Verified and decision time; the use case ends.

**Alternate Flows:**

**A1 — GPS location is unavailable** (at step 12)
- 12a. Location / GPS Service returns no location (location services are off, or there is no fix in time).
- 12b. System displays "GPS location is unavailable" and the option Enter location manually.
- 12c. Citizen places the pin on the map at the observed spot (or types the coordinates).
- 12d. System validates the location (within Sri Lanka) and displays the location marked "Entered manually", with no accuracy figure; continues at step 15. (The district is set from this location at step 18.)

**A2 — Network connection is unavailable: Save Data Offline** (at step 18; extension point `network unavailable`; Save Data Offline «include»s Synchronise Offline Data, which runs when the connection is restored)
- 18a. System sets the report's district from the location as in step 18 (Kalutara) and finds that the phone has no network connection.
- 18b. System saves the draft in the phone's offline report store with the status "Pending synchronisation", keeping the photo copy, the location, the district and the original capture time (07:40).
- 18c. System displays the confirmation screen in its offline state: "Saved on this phone — Pending synchronisation", the capture time and "It will be sent automatically when the app is open and the connection returns". No report ID is shown, because the server assigns it on receipt. The report also appears under My reports — waiting on this phone as "Pending synchronisation".
- 18d. When the connection is restored while the app is open (08:20), or when the app is next opened or returns to the foreground with a connection, System sends each "Pending synchronisation" draft to the server. Each draft goes with its draft reference as the submission ID, its original capture time and the number of other drafts still Pending synchronisation (Rejected drafts are not counted).
- 18e. System (server) validates the report again, confirms that the submission has not been received before and records it as in step 19, with capture time 07:40 and received time 08:20.
- 18f. System removes the draft from the offline report store and displays the synchronisation result, whose message carries the report ID: "Report sent — RPT-2026-KLT-0847, Pending verification"; continues at step 21 (the times in the following steps are later accordingly).
- 18g. If the server had already recorded this submission (the reply to an earlier attempt was lost), it returns the recorded report instead of creating a second one. System removes the draft and shows the same report ID in the synchronisation result's message; continues at step 21.

**A3 — Citizen edits the report before submission** (at step 17)
- 17a. Citizen finds a mistake on the review screen and selects Edit.
- 17b. System returns to the report form with every value kept (hazard type, description, photo, location).
- 17c. Citizen corrects the details, for example the hazard type or the description, or replaces the photo.
- 17d. Citizen selects Review Report; continues at step 16.

**A4 — Duty officer rejects the report** (at step 25; extension point `report rejected`; Reject Report «include»s Record Rejection Reason)
- 25a. DMC Duty Officer judges the report invalid, unreliable or a duplicate and selects Reject.
- 25b. System displays the mandatory rejection-reason field.
- 25c. DMC Duty Officer enters the reason, written in the reporter's language (Sinhala; shown here in English translation): "Duplicate of RPT-2026-KLT-0843 — same stretch of the river, 35 minutes earlier; please report only new changes."
- 25d. DMC Duty Officer confirms the rejection when asked "Reject RPT-2026-KLT-0847 with this reason?".
- 25e. System records the rejection: status Rejected, the reason, the rejecting officer (K. Perera) and the decision time (08:15).
- 25f. Once the rejection is recorded, System sends the decision notice with the reason, exactly as the officer typed it, to the reporter as a push notification in the reporter's preferred language (by SMS as in A9 when push is not possible).
- 25g. System displays on the console "Report rejected" with the report ID, the reason, the officer and the time.
- 25h. Citizen's phone displays the notice with the reason. The report-status screen shows the status Rejected and the reason. The use case ends.

**A5 — Duty officer needs further review before deciding** (at step 25)
- 25i. DMC Duty Officer cannot yet judge the report, for example because the photo is unclear.
- 25j. DMC Duty Officer compares it with the nearby reports shown at step 24 and returns to the queue without deciding.
- 25k. System keeps the report in the queue with the status Pending verification.
- 25l. DMC Duty Officer later selects the report again; continues at step 23.

**A6 — No existing hazard fits: verify as a new hazard** (at step 25)
- 25m. No candidate hazard fits the report. For example, a Blocked road report, RPT-2026-KLT-0852, on the Horana–Ratnapura road at Ingiriya has no recorded blocked-road hazard nearby.
- 25n. DMC Duty Officer selects Verify as New Hazard and enters the severity (Medium), the districts (Kalutara), the place name ("Horana–Ratnapura road at Ingiriya") and a description. (Missing districts, severity or place name: E9. A report already decided: E10.)
- 25o. System creates the hazard with the report's hazard type and location, the officer's severity, districts, place name and description, the source Verified report, the start time equal to the report's capture time and the status Active. It then records the verification: status Verified, the link to the new hazard, the verifying officer and the time.
- 25p. System finds that the new hazard has no active warning.
- 25q. Continues at step 27. At step 29 the extension point is not reached, so the flow continues at step 30.

**A7 — Verified report affects a hazard with an active warning: escalation prompt** (at step 29; extension point `verified report affects an active hazard`; Escalate Warning, its own use case)
- 29a. System displays the escalation prompt: "Flood — Kalu Ganga at Ratnapura has an active warning — WRN-2026-0087, Level 2 – Amber (Be Alert), Ratnapura and Kalutara, valid until 21 May 2026 10:45. Escalate this warning?", with Escalate and Not now.
- 29b. DMC Duty Officer selects Escalate (08:16); this choice is Escalate Warning main flow, step 3, where Escalate Warning starts.
- 29c. The escalation screen opens for WRN-2026-0087 and the flow continues in Escalate Warning (main flow from step 3), a separate use case: the officer raises the warning to Level 3 – Red (Evacuate) with a new message, new instructions (each in Sinhala, Tamil and English) and a new expiry time, previews the re-broadcast and confirms it. The verification is already recorded and does not depend on the escalation. Continues at step 30.
- 29d. If the officer selects Not now instead, System closes the prompt and changes nothing; continues at step 30.

**A8 — App opened without a connection** (at step 2)
- 2a. System cannot reach the DMC server.
- 2b. System displays the last home screen saved on the phone, marked "Offline", with My reports — waiting on this phone (any drafts on this phone). Report Hazard stays available. If no home has been saved yet (first launch while offline), the home shows only the drafts on this phone and the Offline mark.
- 2c. The language selector is disabled while the home screen comes from the phone's saved copy, so the preferred language cannot be changed offline (steps 3–4 are skipped).
- 2d. Continues at step 5 (the report is then saved offline at step 18, A2).

**A9 — Decision notice sent by SMS** (at step 27; also at step 25f)
- 27a. The reporter has no app device registered, or the Push Notification Service reports that the push failed.
- 27b. System sends the same notice by SMS through the SMS Gateway (mobile operator), in the reporter's preferred language.
- 27c. Continues at step 28; at step 30 the reporter reads the notice as an SMS. (From 25f: continues at 25g; at 25h the reporter reads the notice as an SMS.)

**Postconditions:**
- A submitted report is recorded once, with a server-assigned report ID, the hazard type, the description, one photo, the location (GPS with its accuracy, or entered manually), the district (resolved from the location, else the reporter's registered district), the capture time, the received time and the reporter. Its status is Pending verification until the duty officer decides.
- A report captured without a connection stays on the phone as "Pending synchronisation" until the connection returns. It is then recorded as above with its original capture time, and replaying it never creates a second report. A draft the server refused stays on the phone as Rejected with the reason until the citizen corrects and resubmits it (with the same draft reference) or discards it.
- A verified report has the status Verified and is linked to an existing hazard or to a new hazard (source Verified report, in the districts and with the severity the officer gave). The verifying officer and the verification time are recorded.
- A rejected report has the status Rejected, with its mandatory reason, the rejecting officer and the decision time.
- A report is decided at most once: a second decision on a report that is no longer pending is refused and the recorded decision is shown (E10).
- No warning is issued or changed by this use case. When the linked hazard had an active warning, the officer was offered Escalate Warning (after E10 following an earlier attempt that had in fact been recorded, the officer can escalate it from the warning dashboard, Escalate Warning A1).
- The decision notice was sent to the reporter (push, or SMS as the fallback) in their preferred language, with the reason when the report is rejected, and the reporter can see the decision on the report-status screen. If both push and SMS fail, the reporter is not notified and sees the decision only on the report-status screen (a stated limitation).
- The phone's last-synchronisation record is updated on every upload and replay; a failed replay attempt is counted on the draft (E4). The citizen's preferred language is updated when it was changed (online only).

**Exception Flows:**

**E1 — Required report information is missing or invalid** (at step 16)
- 16a. Validation finds a problem in the draft, for example an empty description or a description longer than 500 characters.
- 16b. System stays on the report form, keeping the values entered, and displays the validation messages beside the fields concerned (a photo problem is shown as the photo error, as in E2).
- 16c. Citizen completes or corrects the fields and selects Review Report; continues at step 16.

**E2 — Photograph cannot be attached** (at step 11; 11d at step 10)
- 11a. The chosen image is unreadable or is not a JPEG or PNG image.
- 11b. System's draft validation returns the photo error ("photo: not a readable JPEG or PNG image"); System attaches no photo and displays the photo error "This image cannot be used — choose a JPEG or PNG photo".
- 11c. Citizen takes another photo or chooses another image; continues at step 11.
- 11d. If instead the camera permission is refused at step 10, the app screen itself shows "Allow camera access to take the required photo" (no draft check is involved); the citizen grants the permission or chooses an image from the gallery; continues at step 10.

**E3 — Invalid location** (at step 12d of A1)
- 12e. The manually entered location lies outside Sri Lanka or cannot be read.
- 12f. System displays the location error and asks for the location again; continues at step 12c.

**E4 — Offline synchronisation fails** (at step 18d of A2)
- 18h. The upload fails because the connection drops or the server returns a server error (5xx), so no receipt arrives.
- 18i. System records the failed attempt (the draft's attempt count goes up by one) and keeps the draft as "Pending synchronisation", so no data is lost: the phone has no receipt, so it keeps the draft; if the server had in fact recorded it, the replay returns the recorded report (18g). My reports — waiting on this phone still shows it as "Pending synchronisation". System tries again the next time the connection is restored or the app is opened with a connection (while the connection stays up, the app retries when it next returns to the foreground); continues at step 18d.

**E5 — Service unavailable while recording the decision** (at step 26; also at steps 25e and 25o)
- 26a. The decision cannot be recorded because of a server or network failure (5xx, timeout or unreachable).
- 26b. System shows that the service is unavailable and nothing was changed: the report stays Pending verification, no hazard is created and no notice is sent.
- 26c. DMC Duty Officer retries the decision; continues at step 25. If the first attempt had in fact recorded the decision, the report is no longer pending and the retry follows E10.

**E6 — Server refuses the online submission** (at step 19)
- 19a. The server's validation fails, for example because the photo arrived unreadable.
- 19b. System (server) records no report and replies that the report was not accepted, with the reason Validation failed and the message.
- 19c. System displays the confirmation screen in its "not accepted" state with the errors and returns to the review screen; continues at step 16.
- 19d. If the upload instead fails with a server or network failure (5xx, timeout or unreachable — no receipt arrives), the phone has no receipt, so it keeps the draft: because the submission is offline-capable, System saves the draft as "Pending synchronisation" as in 18b–18c; if the server had in fact recorded it, the replay returns the recorded report (18g); continues at step 18d when the connection returns.

**E7 — Rejection reason missing** (at step 25d of A4)
- 25r. DMC Duty Officer confirms the rejection with an empty reason.
- 25s. System refuses the rejection, records nothing and displays "A rejection reason is required"; continues at step 25c.

**E8 — Server rejects a replayed report** (at step 18e of A2)
- 18j. The server's validation of the replayed report fails, for example because the photo arrived unreadable; the server replies REJECTED (Validation failed).
- 18k. System marks the draft Rejected with the server's reason. It shows "Report not accepted: <reason>" in the synchronisation results and keeps the draft in My reports — waiting on this phone as Rejected with its reason.
- 18l. Citizen opens the rejected draft from My reports — waiting on this phone.
- 18m. System loads the draft from the phone's offline report store and displays it on the report form with its values and the rejection reason.
- 18n. Citizen corrects the draft (replaces the photo) and selects Review Report; continues at step 16. The corrected draft keeps its draft reference; when it is submitted, the server processes it as a new submission, because only accepted submissions are recorded as processed, and System removes the draft from the offline report store once the server accepts it (step 19, or 18e–18f if it is queued again).
- 18o. If the citizen chooses Discard on the rejected draft instead, System removes the draft from the phone's offline report store and from My reports — waiting on this phone; nothing is sent; the use case ends.

**E9 — Invalid new-hazard details** (at step 25n of A6)
- 25t. DMC Duty Officer selects Verify as New Hazard without giving the districts (or without the severity or the place name).
- 25u. System refuses the decision, creates no hazard, records nothing and displays the validation messages ("Districts are required"); the entered details stay on the form.
- 25v. DMC Duty Officer completes the details and selects Verify as New Hazard again; continues at step 25o.

**E10 — Report already decided** (at step 26; also at step 25e of A4 and step 25o of A6; and on a retry from E5 26c)
- 26d. When System is about to record the decision, it finds that the report is no longer Pending verification: another duty officer has already decided it (also when two officers decide at the same moment: the later decision is refused), or an earlier attempt that seemed to fail (E5) had in fact been recorded.
- 26e. System refuses the second decision, records nothing, creates no hazard and sends no notice. It displays "This report has already been decided" with the recorded status (Verified or Rejected) and the decision time.
- 26f. DMC Duty Officer returns to the queue, which no longer offers the report; if the recorded decision linked a hazard with an active warning, the officer can escalate it from the warning dashboard (Escalate Warning A1); continues at step 22.
